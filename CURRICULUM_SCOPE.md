## 6th Grade Math (SAVVAS — year at a glance)
1. Numbers & Operations: multiplying/dividing fractions, fluency with decimals
2. Integers & Rational Numbers: integer operations
3. Expressions/Equations/Relationships: variables & expressions → equivalent expressions → equations & inequalities → two-variable relationships
4. Proportionality: ratios, rates, ratio reasoning
5. Geometry: area, volume, triangles & triangle properties
6. Measurement & Data: data displays, measures of center & variation
7. Personal Financial Literacy: banking, credit, paying for college, annual salaries

## 7th Grade Math (Pearson — year at a glance)
1. Numbers & Operations: rational numbers; opposites & absolute value; +/−/×/÷ rational numbers
2. Expressions/Equations/Relationships: solving simple equations; writing & solving two-step equations; solving simple inequalities; writing problem situations
3. Measurement & Geometry: measuring angles; adjacent/complementary/supplementary/vertical angles; triangle angle sum; center/radius/diameter; circumference & area of circles; semicircles & quarter circles; composite figures; nets; surface area & volume of right prisms; surface area & volume of right pyramids
4. Proportionality: ratios & rates; proportional relationships; representing linear relationships; percents
5. Data: sampling; comparing two populations; probability concepts; compound events
6. Financial Literacy: economic thinking
7. Step-up: percents >100% & <1%, percent error, expanding & factoring expressions, distributive property equations, multi-step inequalities

- T2/T3 Functions & Linear Functions (taught mixed): rate of change & slope (3B); slope-intercept form (2B); direct variation (2D); point-slope form (2B); standard form (2B); graphing a function rule (3C); writing a function rule (2C); formalizing relations & functions (12A); function notation (2A); parallel & perpendicular lines (2E,2F); transformations of linear functions (3E); scatter plots & trend lines (4C)
- T4 Systems: graphing (3F); substitution (5C); elimination (5C); applications (2I); linear inequalities (3D); systems of inequalities (3H)
- T5 Exponents & Radicals: zero & negative exponents (11B); multiplying powers same base (11B); more multiplication properties (11B); division properties (11B); rational exponents & radicals (11B); simplifying radicals (11A)
- T6 Sequences: arithmetic & geometric (12D); arithmetic recursive (12C); geometric recursive (12C)
- T7 Polynomials & Factoring: add/subtract (10A); multiply & factoring (10D); multiplying binomials (10B); factoring x²+bx+c (10E); factoring ax²+bx+c (10E); special cases (10B/10F); grouping (10B); simplifying rational expressions (10D)
- T8 Quadratics: graphs & properties (6A); quadratic functions (7A); transformations (7C); vertex form (6B); solving (8A); factoring to solve (8A); writing (6C); completing the square (8A); quadratic formula & discriminant (8A)
- T9 Exponentials: exponential functions (9A); growth & decay (9B); modeling data (9E)

## Geometry — Topics 1–15 (lesson-level; TEKS column blank in doc — use TEKS G.*)
- T1 Tools of Geometry: points/lines/planes; measuring segments; measuring angles; angle pairs; basic constructions; constructing parallel & perpendicular lines
- T2 Reasoning & Proof: patterns & conjectures; conditional statements; biconditional statements; deductive reasoning; reasoning in algebra & geometry; proving angles congruent
- T3 Parallel & Perpendicular Lines: lines & angles; properties of parallel lines; proving lines parallel; parallel & perpendicular lines; parallel lines & triangles; equations of lines in coordinate plane; slopes of parallel & perpendicular lines
- T4 Congruent Triangles: congruent figures; SSS & SAS; ASA & AAS; CPCTC (corresponding parts); isosceles & equilateral triangles; HL (right triangles)
- T5 Relationships Within Triangles: midpoint & distance; midsegments; perpendicular & angle bisectors; bisectors in triangles; medians & altitudes; inequalities in one/two triangles
- T6 Polygons & Quadrilaterals: polygon angle-sum; parallelograms (properties + proving); rhombuses/rectangles/squares (properties + conditions); trapezoids & kites
- T7 Coordinate Geometry: polygons in coordinate plane; applying coordinate geometry; proofs using coordinate geometry
- T8 Transformational Geometry: translations; reflections; rotations; symmetry; compositions; congruence transformations; dilations; non-rigid transformations
- T9 Similarity: similar polygons; similarity transformations; proving triangles similar; similarity in right triangles; proportions in triangles
- T10 Right Triangles & Trig: Pythagorean theorem & converse; special right triangles; trigonometry; angles of elevation & depression
- T11 Circle Measurement: circles & arcs; radian measure; areas of circles & sectors; circles in coordinate plane
- T12 Circle Theorems: tangent lines; chords & arcs; inscribed angles; angle measures & segment lengths
- T13 Area: parallelograms & triangles; trapezoids/rhombuses/kites; regular polygons; perimeters & areas of similar figures; trigonometry & area
- T14 Surface Area & Volume: 3D figures & cross sections; SA prisms & cylinders; SA pyramids & cones; volumes prisms & cylinders; volumes pyramids & cones; spheres; related solids
- T15 Probability: experimental & theoretical; geometric probability; permutations & combinations; compound probability; conditional probability (frequency tables + formulas)
